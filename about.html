About Me

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, where I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, education, and human-computer interaction.

My research focuses on how people learn with and interact with AI, particularly in educational settings. As a Graduate Research Assistant in the EPIC Lab, I contribute to the Appalachian AI Corps project, working on AI literacy education, educational technology, curriculum development, and mixed-methods research with rural high school students.

I'm particularly interested in human-centered AI, AI ethics, educational technology, and designing technology that is useful and accessible to the people who use it.

Beyond Research

I also enjoy learning by building things. I'm currently interested in developing my skills in data visualization, science communication, and game development, and I'm using personal projects to explore these areas.

Outside of research and technical projects, I enjoy collaborating with others and helping build welcoming communities in computer science. I've served in leadership roles in student organizations supporting women and gender-diverse students in computing.

What I'm Interested In

  • Human-centered artificial intelligence
  • AI ethics and AI literacy
  • Human-computer interaction
  • Educational technology
  • Data visualization
  • Science communication
  • Interactive and game-based experiences

Outside the Lab

When I'm not working, I tend to be writing - not academic papers, but personal projects. I enjoy creating sci-fi, fantasy, and horror stories. I also love art, learning languages, gaming, and being creative in other ways, and try to bring this into my work wherever I can.
